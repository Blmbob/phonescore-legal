The badge that proves you check

In the used-device market, everyone promises clean devices. The Certified badge isn't promised: it's earned, check after check, and it's lost if you stop.

Why a badge

A serious reseller checks their devices before selling them. The problem is, their customer has no way to know it: nothing sets them apart from a reseller who checks nothing. Both say the same thing.

The badge answers that. It can't be bought or self-declared: it's based on your real checks, recorded by PhoneScore. A shop that carries it has checked at least ten devices in the last thirty days. That's verifiable, and that's what gives it its value.

How to get it

Certified ✓

Active reseller access and 10 checks in 30 days. Your shop appears in the app directory, badge visible to every buyer.

Member

Active reseller access, fewer than 10 checks. Your shop is visible, without the badge. Every check brings you closer to the threshold.

None

Expired reseller access, or no checks. The shop does not appear in the directory.

Reseller access is obtained with the Reseller pack: 10 coins for 6,700 FCFA, which unlock 30 days of access. Those ten coins are exactly enough to run the ten checks for the threshold.

It stays the way it was earned

The count runs over the last 30 rolling days, not the calendar month. So there is no cramming: you cannot make up for a slow month by bunching your checks on the last day. The badge follows your real activity, day after day, and is recalculated every night.

If you drop below the threshold, the badge falls back to Member. It comes back on its own as soon as you cross above it again: nothing to request, nothing to pay.

So you never lose it without seeing it coming, the My Shop screen always shows where you stand: "7 checks in the last 30 days — 3 more for the badge".

What reseller access gives you

  • Your stock — record your devices, track what is in stock, reserved, or sold, and find an IMEI by search.
  • Your invoices — generated at the sale, numbered, with a QR code linking to the device's verification report.
  • Your archive — sold devices stay viewable with their entry and exit dates, useful during an inspection.
  • Up to three shops — to keep stocks separate. Only one is primary: it is the one that receives your checks and carries the badge.

Getting started

Reseller mode activates from the app, after purchasing the Reseller pack. You then create your shop, and your checks count from that moment on.

A question about the badge or your shop? Write to us at support@phonescore.app.
