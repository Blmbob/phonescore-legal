PhoneScore

Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy explains what data PhoneScore collects, why, and how it is protected. It applies to the PhoneScore mobile application, published in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. For any question: support@phonescore.app.

In brief

  • Your data is never sold or rented, and is never used for advertising.
  • No banking data reaches us: Apple and GeniusPay collect payment, we only receive a transaction reference.
  • An IMEI number identifies a device, not a person.
  • You delete your own account, from the app, without writing to us.
  • Shop, location, and reseller tools collect nothing until you use them.

2

Purposes

›

Running the service and replying to you. Nothing is sold, rented, or used for advertising.
  • Provide the service: run checks and display your reports and history
  • Manage your account, your coin balance, and your purchases
  • Automatically credit your coins once a payment is confirmed
  • Display your shop in the public directory and, if you asked, place it on the map
  • Provide the reseller tools: stock tracking, invoicing, and sales statistics
  • Reply to you when you contact support
  • Prevent fraud and abuse of the service

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising purposes.

4

Retention

›

Your data remains for as long as the account exists. You can remove some of it at any time from the app.
  • Account data: kept for as long as the account is active
  • Check and transaction history: kept for as long as the account is active, so you can review your past reports
  • Shop, stock, and invoices: kept for as long as the account is active. You can remove your shop from the directory or delete a stock entry at any time from the app
4 bis

Deleting your account

›

From the app, Profile screen. Immediate and irreversible.

You can delete your account at any time from the app, without contacting support: Profile screen → "Delete my account".

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. This erases: your account and login credentials, your profile, your remaining coins, your check history and associated reports, your shops and their content (stock, invoices, subscriptions, reviews received), and your purchase history.

Only already-anonymized data that no longer identifies you is kept: the amount of any commission owed to a partner for a promo code used, with no link to your account. Your unused coins are lost and are not refunded.

5

Security

›

Encrypted traffic, hashed passwords, each account isolated in the database.

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed. Access to data is restricted by database-level security rules (Row Level Security): each user can only access their own data. Access keys for third-party services are stored server-side and never pass through the app.

6

Your rights

›

Deletion happens in the app. For other rights, write to us: reply within 30 days.

Under Ivorian law n° 2013-450 of June 19, 2013 on the protection of personal data, you have a right of access, rectification, deletion, and objection over your data.

The right of deletion is exercised directly from the app (see section 4 bis), without writing to us. For other rights, write to support@phonescore.app from the email address linked to your account. We reply within 30 days at the latest.

You may also file a complaint with ARTCI (the Telecommunications/ICT Regulatory Authority of Côte d'Ivoire), the personal data protection authority in Côte d'Ivoire.

7

The service is not intended for people under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us to have it deleted.
