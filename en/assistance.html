Support

Frequently asked questions, and how to reach us.

How do I find a phone's IMEI?

Dial *#06# on the device's phone keypad. The 15 digits appear immediately. The IMEI is also found in Settings → General → About.

How do coins work?

One coin covers an iPhone check, two coins a MacBook check. Coins are only deducted once the check succeeds.

My check failed — are my coins lost?

No. If a check fails for a technical reason, the coin is automatically returned to your account. If that didn't happen, write to us with the date and the IMEI involved, and we'll fix it.

My balance doesn't show up after a purchase

Reopen the app and wait a few seconds — the balance syncs with our servers. If a purchase was interrupted, it resumes automatically the next time you open the app. If it is still wrong after a few minutes, contact us with the date and amount of the payment.

How are coins billed?

On iPhone and iPad, packs are sold through the App Store, at the price shown in the app at the time of purchase. Refund requests go through Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com, not through us: we have no ability to refund an App Store purchase.

How do I list my shop?

Listing in the directory is reserved for holders of the Reseller Pack (10 coins). The purchase unlocks 30 days of reseller access, which grants a spot on the map, stock tracking, and invoicing. After that period, a new pack extends access; your shop and data stay in place in the meantime.

The verdict is red — what should I do?

A red verdict flags a device that is iCloud-locked, blacklisted, or carrier-locked. We recommend not going through with the purchase. A locked device cannot be unlocked by a reseller.

How do I delete my account?

Directly from the app, without going through support: Profile screen → "Delete my account". Deletion is immediate and irreversible. This erases your account, your remaining coins, your check history, and your shop and its content if you had one.

Support, complaints, and account deletion

support@phonescore.app

Reply within 48 business hours. Write to us in French or English.

For any complaint, please include if possible:

  • the email address on your account;
  • the date and time of the issue;
  • the IMEI involved, if relevant.
