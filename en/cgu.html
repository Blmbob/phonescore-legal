2. Purpose of the service

PhoneScore checks the history of used Apple devices (iPhone and MacBook) from their IMEI or serial number. Depending on the information available, the verification report may include: iCloud lock status (Find My iPhone), blacklist status (device reported stolen or lost), carrier lock status, and the device's model and specifications.

The Service is aimed at buyers and resellers of used devices who want to reduce the risk of acquiring a stolen, locked, or misdescribed device.
