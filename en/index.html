What PhoneScore checks

From the IMEI alone, against the manufacturer and GSMA databases.

  • iCloud lock — the costliest issue: a device tied to "Find My" is unusable, and no one can unlock it.
  • Reported stolen or lost — GSMA blacklist entry. A flagged device can be cut off the network at any time.
  • Carrier lock — a SIM-locked phone only accepts its original carrier, often a foreign one.
  • Real model and capacity — what was actually manufactured under this IMEI, regardless of what the seller claims.
  • SIM cards and eSIM — US iPhones since the 14 have no SIM tray at all. The report flags it.
  • Warranty — purchase date and remaining Apple coverage, when the information exists.
  • Shareable report — send it to the seller or buyer over WhatsApp.
